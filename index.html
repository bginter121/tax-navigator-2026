Owner Wage Coordination and Adjustments

Use exact W-2 Box 3 and Box 5 amounts for payroll-tax coordination. Health-insurance and retirement entries must already be deductible amounts.

Primary Client

i

Why this section exists

These entries do not add income. They help coordinate payroll tax limits when a business owner has both W-2 wages and Schedule C profit.

What should I enter?

  • W-2 Box 3: Social Security wages already reported on the owner's W-2. This helps avoid overstating the Social Security portion of self-employment tax once the wage base is used.
  • W-2 Box 5: Medicare wages and tips already reported on the owner's W-2. Medicare has no wage cap, so this can be higher than Box 3.
  • Use the actual owner-specific W-2 amounts when available. Do not enter W-2 Box 1 wages here.

If the owner has no W-2 wages, leave both boxes blank or zero. Health-insurance and retirement entries should be advisor-determined deductible amounts.

Source: IRS W-2 instructions

Spouse

Confirm W-2 Box 3 and Box 5 wages for each business owner. A zero entry can overstate Social Security tax or understate Additional Medicare Tax.
